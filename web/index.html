Storage and privacy

SQLite database and the separate browser profile live outside the vault. The profile may retain your manual login locally. The recorder does not read credentials, bets, balances or wallets. The website and dependency downloads still require internet.

Your own Obsidian sync, Git, or publication settings can transmit files exported into your vault. Do not share the browser profile.

Capture events

Keep the recorder, capture browser and computer awake with an active connection. Being online in another browser alone does not keep this collector running.
